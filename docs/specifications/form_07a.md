# Form 7A

**Official Name:** Representation for Opposition to Grant of Patent

**Purpose:** Used to file a **pre-grant** representation by way of opposition to the grant of a
patent, in respect of a published application, stating the representor's particulars and the
grounds.

**Legal Reference:**
- The Patents Act, 1970 (39 of 1970) — (pre-grant representation, Section 25(1))
- The Patents Rules, 2003 — **Rule 55**

**Page count:** 1

---

## Overall Layout Notes

Two columns: left = numbered instructions (1–5); right = the form. Structurally parallel to Form 7
but for **pre-grant** representation against a *published application* (there is no granted patent
number yet).

---

## Sections

### Section 1 — Representor Particulars *(instruction 1: state names, address and nationality)*

**Field 1.1 — I/We (representor: names, address, nationality)**
- Field Type: Multi-line Text (composite) · Required: Mandatory · Repeatable: Yes
- Auto-fill: **Partial** — representor is usually a third party. Validation: Strike-out Choice `I/We`.

### Section 2 — Application Being Opposed

Pre-printed: "hereby give representation by way of opposition to the grant of patent in respect of
application no …… dated …… made by …… and published on ……"

- **Field 2.1 — Application No.** — Text · Mandatory · Auto-fill: from Form 1 / publication record.
- **Field 2.2 — Application dated** — Date · Mandatory · Auto-fill: from Form 1.
- **Field 2.3 — Made by (applicant)** — Multi-line Text · Mandatory · Auto-fill: applicant name.
- **Field 2.4 — Published on (date)** — Date · Mandatory · Auto-fill: from publication record.

### Section 3 — Grounds *(instruction 2: state the grounds one after another)*

**Field 3.1 — Grounds of representation/opposition**
- Field Type: Multi-line Text · Required: Mandatory · Repeatable: Yes (one per ground) · Auto-fill: No.

### Section 4 — Address for Service *(instruction 3: complete address incl postal index number/code
and state along with telephone and fax number)*

**Field 4.1 — My/our address for service in India**
- Field Type: Address / Multi-line Text · Required: Mandatory · Auto-fill: from representor's agent.

### Section 5 — Signature & Signatory

- Instruction 4: "To be signed by the opponent or by his/her authorized registered patent agent."
- Instruction 5 / **Field 5.1 — Name and designation of the natural person who has signed** — Text ·
  Mandatory. (Note: Form 7A asks for **name and designation**, unlike Form 7's name only.)
- See Signature Block.

### Footer — Addressee
`To — The Controller of Patents, The Patent Office, At ……`
- **Field F.1 — Patent Office location** — Dropdown (Delhi / Mumbai / Chennai / Kolkata). Mandatory.

## Signature Block

- **Signature** — Type: Signature. Mandatory. Signed by the opponent or his/her authorised
  registered patent agent.
- **Name and designation of the natural person who has signed** — Type: Text. Mandatory
  (both name **and** designation).
- **Date** — no explicit "Dated this…" line printed; do not invent.
- **Place** — not printed; do not invent.

---

## Special Notes

- **Fee:** No fee is indicated on the face of Form 7A. Pre-grant representation under Rule 55 is
  historically **without fee** — confirm against the current First Schedule before enforcing.
- **Strike-out:** `I/We`, `My/our`, `his/her`.
- **Distinguish from Form 7:** Form 7A is **pre-grant** (published application, no patent number);
  Form 7 is **post-grant** (granted patent number).
- **"Name and designation":** capture both, unlike Form 7 (name only).
- **Attachments:** statement and evidence in support of the representation typically accompany.
