# Form 22

**Official Name:** Application for Registration of Patent Agent

**Purpose:** Used by an individual to apply for **registration as a patent agent**, enclosing a
character certificate, declaring freedom from disqualifications, and providing personal details
and qualifications.

**Legal Reference:**
- The Patents Act, 1970 (39 of 1970)
- The Patents Rules, 2003 — **Rules 109(1) and 112**

**Page count:** 1

---

## Overall Layout Notes

Two columns: left = numbered instructions (1–5); right = the form. The applicant here is a
**prospective patent agent** (an individual), not a patent applicant — so auto-fill from uploaded
patent documents is largely **not applicable**; this form is filled from the person's own details.

---

## Sections

### Section 1 — Application & Character Certificate

- **Field 1.0 — Opening** — Static/Boilerplate: "I beg to apply for registration as a patent agent
  under the Patents Act, 1970."
- **Field 1.1 — Character certificate from** *(marker ¹; instruction 1)* — Text · Mandatory · Auto-fill: No.
  Instruction 1: the certificate testifying to the character of the applicant should be from a person
  not related to him and being a Gazetted Officer or any other person whom the Controller thinks fit.
  "A certificate of character From …… is enclosed herewith." (Implies an attachment.)

### Section 2 — Declaration of No Disqualification

**Field 2.1 — Declaration** — Static/Boilerplate: "I hereby declare that I am not subject to any of
the dis-qualifications specified in rule 114 of the Patents Rules 2003 and that the information given
below is true to the best of my knowledge and belief." Required: Mandatory.

## Signature Block

- **Date** — `Dated this … day of … 20_` — Type: Date. Mandatory.
- **Signature (marker ⁴, instruction 4)** — Type: Signature. Mandatory. Signed by the **applicant**
  personally (not an agent — the applicant *is* the prospective agent).
- **Name of the natural person who has signed (marker ⁵, instruction 5)** — Type: Text. Mandatory.
- **Place** — not printed; do not invent.

---

## Special Notes

- **Note (a):** "For fee : See First Schedule." A prescribed fee applies.
- **Note (b):** "Attach two recent passport size photographs." → attachment.
- **Note (c):** "Provide specimen signature in separate sheet." → attachment.
- **Attachments required:** character certificate (from an unrelated Gazetted Officer / person the
  Controller thinks fit), original certificates/attested copies of qualification documents
  (instruction 3), two passport photos, specimen signature sheet.
- **Not a patent-applicant form:** auto-fill from uploaded patent documents does **not** apply; this
  is filled from the prospective agent's own details. The app may still store and reuse these details.
- **Sensitive PII:** date/place of birth, father's name, residential address, photographs — treat as
  personal data.
