# Form 28

**Official Name:** To Be Submitted by a Small Entity / Startup / Educational Institution

**Purpose:** Used by an applicant/patentee to **declare and prove** their status as a small entity,
a startup, or an educational institution — the status that determines reduced official fees.

**Legal Reference:**
- The Patents Act, 1970 (39 of 1970)
- The Patents Rules, 2003 — **Rules 2(fa), 2(fb), 2(ca) and 7**

**Page count:** 1

---

## Overall Layout Notes

Two-column table with four numbered rows. Section 2 is a **reference table** of the evidence required
for each claimable status (small entity / startup / educational institution).

---

## Sections

### Section 1 — Declarant & Status Claim

Pre-printed: "I/We …… **applicant/patentee** in respect of the patent application no …… or patent no
…… hereby declare that I/we am/are **a small entity in accordance with rule 2(fa) or a startup in
accordance with rule 2(fb) or an educational institution in accordance with rule 2(ca)** and submit
the following document(s) as proof:"

- **Field 1.1 — Capacity** — Strike-out Choice (`applicant` | `patentee`) · Mandatory · Auto-fill: **Partial**.
- **Field 1.2 — Name, address, nationality** *(instruction 1)* — Multi-line Text (composite) · Mandatory ·
  Auto-fill: **Yes** — from Form 1 / Certificate.
- **Field 1.3 — Patent application no. / Patent no.** — Text · Mandatory · Auto-fill: **Yes** (Strike-out `application no / patent no`).
- **Field 1.4 — Status claimed** — Radio Button (`small entity (rule 2(fa))` | `startup (rule 2(fb))` |
  `educational institution (rule 2(ca))`) · Mandatory · Auto-fill: **Partial** — from Form 1 §3B category.

### Section 2 — Documents to be Submitted → see **Table T1**.

### Section 3 — Declaration & Date

- **Field 3.1 — Declaration** — Static/Boilerplate: "The information provided herein is correct to the
  best of my/our knowledge and belief." Required: Mandatory.
- **Field 3.2 — Dated this __ day of __ 20__** — Date · Mandatory · Auto-fill: derived.
- Instruction 3: "To be signed by the applicant(s)/patentee(s)/authorized registered patent agent."

### Section 4 — Signatory
- **Field 4.1 — Name of the natural person who has signed** — Text · Mandatory.
- See Signature Block.

*(Note: Form 28 as printed has no explicit "To, The Controller … at …" addressee block; it is filed
with/attached to the related application. Do not invent an addressee field — but the app may still
record the target office at application level.)*

## Tables

### Table T1 — Evidence by Claimed Status (Section 2)

Reference table: which document proves each status. The row for the Section-1.4 status drives a
required attachment.

| Status | Indian applicant evidence | Foreign entity evidence |
|---|---|---|
| i. Small entity | Evidence of registration under the Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises Development Act, 2006 (27 of 2006) | Any other document |
| ii. Startup | Any document as evidence of eligibility, as defined in rule 2(fb) | Any other document |
| iii. Educational institution | Any document as evidence of eligibility, as defined in rule 2(ca) | Any other document (foreign educational institution) |

- **Row behaviour:** static reference (i–iii). **Multiple rows allowed:** No.

## Signature Block

- **Date (Field 3.2)** — Type: Date. Mandatory.
- **Signature** — Type: Signature. Mandatory. Signed by applicant(s)/patentee(s)/authorised registered patent agent.
- **Name of the natural person who has signed (Field 4.1)** — Type: Text. Mandatory.
- **Place** — not printed; do not invent.

---

## Special Notes

- **Fee impact:** this form establishes fee-reduced status; it does not itself carry a fee note on the
  face. It is closely tied to Form 1 §3B — the claimed status should be consistent across the two.
- **Attachment required:** the status-proof document from Table T1 (matched to the claimed status and
  Indian/foreign applicant).
- **Strike-out:** `applicant/patentee`, `am/are`, `application no/patent no`, and the status clause
  (retain only the claimed status).
