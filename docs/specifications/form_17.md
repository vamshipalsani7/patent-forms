# Form 17

**Official Name:** Application for Compulsory Licence

**Purpose:** Used to apply for the grant of a **compulsory licence** under a patent, stating the
patent particulars, the grounds relied upon, and the documentary evidence of the applicant's
interest.

**Legal Reference:**
- The Patents Act, 1970 (39 of 1970) — **Sections 84(1), 91, 92(1) or 92A**
- The Patents Rules, 2003 — **Rule 96**

**Page count:** 1

---

## Overall Layout Notes

Two columns: left = numbered instructions (1–5); right = the form with superscript markers.

---

## Sections

### Section 1 — Applicant Particulars *(instruction 1: name, address and nationality of the applicant(s))*

**Field 1.1 — I/We (name, address, nationality)** *(marker ¹)*
- Field Type: Multi-line Text (composite) · Required: Mandatory · Repeatable: Yes
- Auto-fill: **Partial** — applicant is often not the patentee. Validation: Strike-out `I/We`.

### Section 2 — Patent Subject to the Application

Pre-printed: "hereby apply for the grant of a compulsory licence under Patent No. …… dated ……
granted to …… for which the patentee is …… on the following grounds, namely : ……"

- **Field 2.1 — Patent No.** — Text · Mandatory · Auto-fill: **Yes** — from Certificate.
- **Field 2.2 — Dated (of the patent)** — Date · Mandatory · Auto-fill: **Yes**.
- **Field 2.3 — Granted to** — Text · Mandatory · Auto-fill: **Yes**.
- **Field 2.4 — Patentee** — Text · Mandatory · Auto-fill: **Yes**.
- **Field 2.5 — Grounds ("on the following grounds, namely:")** — Multi-line Text · Mandatory ·
  Repeatable: Yes · Auto-fill: No.

### Section 3 — Declaration

**Field 3.1 — Declaration** — Static/Boilerplate: "I/We declare that the facts and matters stated
herein are true to the best of my/our knowledge, information and belief." Required: Mandatory.

### Section 4 — Documentary Evidence of Interest *(marker ²)*

Pre-printed: "The details of documentary evidence in support of my/our interest and the grounds
stated above are given below² (a) …… (b) …… (c) ……"
- **Field 4.1 — Evidence item (a)/(b)/(c)** — Text · Mandatory (at least one) · Repeatable: Yes ·
  Auto-fill: **Yes** — list uploaded supporting documents.

### Section 5 — Address for Service *(instruction 3; marker ³)*

**Field 5.1 — My/Our address for service in India** *(complete address incl postal code and state
along with telephone and fax number(s))*
- Field Type: Address / Multi-line Text · Required: Mandatory · Auto-fill: from agent/applicant.

### Section 6 — Date, Signature & Signatory
`Dated this …… day of …… 20_` — see Signature Block.
- Instruction 4: "To be signed by the applicant(s) or by his authorised registered patent agent."
- Instruction 5 / **Field 6.1 — Name of the natural person who has signed** — Text · Mandatory.

### Footer — Addressee
`To — The Controller of Patents, The Patent Office, At ……`
- **Field F.1 — Patent Office location** — Dropdown (Delhi / Mumbai / Chennai / Kolkata). Mandatory.

## Tables

None. (Evidence items (a)–(c) are a short repeatable list, not a printed grid.)

## Signature Block

- **Date** — `Dated this … day of … 20_` — Type: Date. Mandatory.
- **Signature (marker ⁴, instruction 4)** — Type: Signature. Mandatory. Applicant(s) or authorised
  registered patent agent.
- **Name of the natural person who has signed (marker ⁵, instruction 5)** — Type: Text. Mandatory.
- **Place** — not printed; do not invent.

---

## Special Notes

- **Fee (footer):** "For fee : See First Schedule." A prescribed fee applies.
- **Strike-out:** `I/We`, `my/our`.
- **Attachments required:** "Certified copies of the documents are to be enclosed **in duplicate**"
  (instruction 2) — the documentary evidence of interest.
- **Grounds are central:** s.84(1)/91/92(1)/92A each provide distinct grounds; the editor may offer
  ground templates matched to the cited provision, but grounds remain free-text and user-controlled.
