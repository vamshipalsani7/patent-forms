# Form 16

**Official Name:** Application for Registration of Title/Interest in a Patent or Share in It or
Registration of Any Document Purporting to Affect Proprietorship of the Patent

**Purpose:** Used to apply to register the applicant's **title or interest** in a patent (or a
share/interest in it), or to register any document affecting the proprietorship of the patent,
transmitting the document (or an attested/certified copy) as proof.

**Legal Reference:**
- The Patents Act, 1970 (39 of 1970) — **Sections 69(1) and 69(2)**
- The Patents Rules, 2003 — **Rules 90(1) and 90(2)**

**Page count:** 1

---

## Overall Layout Notes

Two columns: left = numbered instructions (1–5); right = the form with superscript markers. The
proof-of-title clause has an **"OR"** branch (transmit the accompanying document + certified copy
/ transmit herewith an attested copy + original for verification).

## Sections

### Section 1 — Applicant Particulars *(instruction 1: name, address and nationality of the applicant(s))*

**Field 1.1 — I/We (name, address, nationality)** *(marker ¹)*
- Field Type: Multi-line Text (composite) · Required: Mandatory · Repeatable: Yes
- Auto-fill: **Yes** — from assignment/transfer docs / applicant records. Validation: Strike-out `I/We`.

### Section 2 — Basis of Registration

Pre-printed: "hereby apply that my/our name(s) may be registered in the register of patent as a
person entitled to the **patent / a share in the patent / an interest in the patent** details of
which are specified below:"

- **Field 2.1 — Entitlement type** — Radio/Strike-out (`patent` | `a share in the patent` |
  `an interest in the patent`) · Mandatory · Auto-fill: No.
- **Field 2.2 — Patent No.** — Text · Mandatory · Auto-fill: **Yes** — from Certificate.
- **Field 2.3 — Dated (of the patent)** — Date · Mandatory · Auto-fill: **Yes**.
- **Field 2.4 — Grantee / Patentee** — Text · Mandatory · Auto-fill: **Yes** (Strike-out `Grantee / Patentee`).

### Section 3 — Proof of Title (choose one — OR branch)

- **Field 3.0 — Proof branch** — Radio Button:
  - Branch A: "in proof thereof we transmit the accompanying …… with a certified copy thereof."
  - Branch B: "Transmit herewith an attested copy of …… in respect of Patent No(s) …… dated ……
    granted to …… of which the patentee is …… as well as the original document for verification …"
- **Field 3.1 — Document transmitted** *(marker ²)* — Text · Mandatory · Auto-fill: **Yes** — name/type of the uploaded title document.
- **Field 3.2 — (Branch B) Patent No(s)** — Text · Conditional · Auto-fill: **Yes**.
- **Field 3.3 — (Branch B) Dated** — Date · Conditional · Auto-fill: **Yes**.
- **Field 3.4 — (Branch B) Granted to / patentee is** — Text · Conditional · Auto-fill: **Yes**.
- **Field 3.5 — Registration request** — Static/Boilerplate: "I/We hereby apply that a notification
  thereof may be entered in the register of patents." Required: Mandatory.

### Section 4 — Address for Service *(instruction 3; marker ³)*

**Field 4.1 — My/Our address for service in India** *(complete address incl postal code and state
along with telephone and fax number(s))*
- Field Type: Address / Multi-line Text · Required: Mandatory · Auto-fill: from agent/applicant.

### Section 5 — Date, Signature & Signatory
`Dated this …… day of …… 20_` — see Signature Block.
- Instruction 4: "To be signed by the applicant or his authorised registered patent agent."
- Instruction 5 / **Field 5.1 — Name of the natural person who has signed** — Text · Mandatory.

### Footer — Addressee
`To — The Controller of Patents, The Patent Office, At ……`
- **Field F.1 — Patent Office location** — Dropdown (Delhi / Mumbai / Chennai / Kolkata). Mandatory.

## Special Notes

- **Note (a):** "For fee : See First Schedule." A prescribed fee applies.
- **Note (b):** "Strike out whichever is not applicable." → entitlement type, OR branch, Grantee/Patentee.
- **Attachments required:** the title/interest document itself (assignment, mortgage, licence, etc.),
  transmitted with a certified copy (Branch A) or as an attested copy plus the original for
  verification (Branch B). Instruction 2 requires a description of the document's nature, date, and
  the parties' names/addresses/nationalities.
- **This is the registration counterpart** to a change of proprietorship; distinct from Form 6
  (change of applicant pre-grant) and Form 10 (amend granted-patent name).
