# Form 23

**Official Name:** Application for the Restoration of the Name in the Register of Patent Agents

**Purpose:** Used by a former patent agent to apply for **restoration of their name** to the register
of patent agents after it was removed, referencing the removal and the original entry.

**Legal Reference:**
- The Patents Act, 1970 (39 of 1970) — **Section 130(2)**
- The Patents Rules, 2003 — **Rule 117(1)**

**Page count:** 1

---

## Overall Layout Notes

Short single-purpose form. A single declarative paragraph, then date/signature. The applicant is the
patent agent (an individual) — not a patent applicant.

---

## Sections

### Section 1 — Restoration Request

Pre-printed: "I, …… hereby apply for the restoration of my name in the register of patent agent which
was removed on …… under section 130 or Rule 116. My name was originally entered in the register on
…… under …… No ……"

- **Field 1.1 — Name of the applicant (agent)** — Text · Mandatory · Auto-fill: **Partial** (from stored agent profile).
- **Field 1.2 — Date of removal** — Date · Mandatory · Auto-fill: No.
- **Field 1.3 — Removal provision** — Strike-out/Radio (`section 130` | `Rule 116`) · Mandatory · Auto-fill: No.
- **Field 1.4 — Date originally entered in the register** — Date · Mandatory · Auto-fill: No.
- **Field 1.5 — Original registration "under …" reference** — Text · Mandatory · Auto-fill: No.
- **Field 1.6 — Registration No.** — Text · Mandatory · Auto-fill: **Partial** (agent's IN/PA no.).

### Section 2 — Date, Signature & Signatory
`Dated this …… day of …… 20_` — see Signature Block.
- Instruction 1: "To be signed by the applicant."
- Instruction 2 / **Field 2.1 — Name of the natural person who has signed** — Text · Mandatory.

### Footer — Addressee
`To — The Controller of Patents, The Patent Office, at ……`
- **Field F.1 — Patent Office location** — Dropdown (Delhi / Mumbai / Chennai / Kolkata). Mandatory.

## Signature Block

- **Date** — `Dated this … day of … 20_` — Type: Date. Mandatory.
- **Signature (marker ¹, instruction 1)** — Type: Signature. Mandatory. Signed by the **applicant**
  (the agent) personally.
- **Name of the natural person who has signed (marker ², instruction 2)** — Type: Text. Mandatory.
- **Place** — not printed; do not invent.

---

## Special Notes

- **Fee (footer):** "For fee : See First Schedule." A prescribed fee applies.
- **Not a patent-applicant form:** relates to the register of patent agents; auto-fill from uploaded
  patent documents does not apply (may reuse a stored agent profile).
- **Removal reference:** capture both the removal date/provision and the original entry details, as
  the form requires the applicant to reference their prior registration.
