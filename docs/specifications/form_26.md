# Form 26

**Official Name:** Form for Authorisation of a Patent Agent / or Any Person in a Matter or Proceeding
Under the Act

**Purpose:** A power of authority by which an applicant/party **authorises a patent agent (or any
person)** to act on their behalf in a specified matter or proceeding, revokes prior authorisations,
and assents to actions already taken.

**Legal Reference:**
- The Patents Act, 1970 (39 of 1970) — **Sections 127 and 132**
- The Patents Rules, 2003 — **Rule 135**

**Page count:** 1

---

## Overall Layout Notes

Two columns: left = numbered instructions (1–5); right = the form with superscript markers. Must be
**stamped** under the Indian Stamp Act, 1899.

---

## Sections

### Section 1 — Authorising Party *(instruction 1: name, address and nationality)*

**Field 1.1 — I/We (name, address, nationality)** *(marker ¹)*
- Field Type: Multi-line Text (composite) · Required: Mandatory · Repeatable: Yes
- Auto-fill: **Yes** — applicant name/address from Form 1 / Specification. Validation: Strike-out `I/We`.

### Section 2 — Authorised Person *(instruction 2: name, address and nationality of the person(s) to be authorized; marker ²)*

**Field 2.1 — Authorised person (name, address, nationality)**
- Field Type: Multi-line Text (composite) · Required: Mandatory · Repeatable: Yes
- Auto-fill: **Partial** — patent agent details if known (IN/PA no., name).

### Section 3 — Matter / Proceeding *(instruction 3: state the particular matter or proceeding; marker ³)*

Pre-printed: "to act on my/our behalf in connection with³ …… and request that all notices,
requisitions and communication relating thereto may be sent to such person at the above address
unless otherwise specified."
- **Field 3.1 — Particular matter or proceeding** — Multi-line Text · Mandatory · Auto-fill: **Partial**
  (e.g. the application number/subject).

### Section 4 — Revocation & Assent Declarations

- **Field 4.1 — Revocation** — Static/Boilerplate: "I/We hereby revoke all previous authorisation, if
  any made, in respect of same matter or proceeding." Required: Mandatory.
- **Field 4.2 — Assent** — Static/Boilerplate: "I/We hereby assent to the action already taken by the
  said person in the above-matter." Required: Mandatory.
- **Field 4.3 — Dated this __ day of __ /20__** — Date · Mandatory · Auto-fill: derived.

### Section 5 — Signature & Signatory
- Instruction 4: "To be signed by the person(s) making this authorisation."
- Instruction 5 / **Field 5.1 — Name of the natural person who has signed, with designation and
  official seal, if any** — Text (+ designation, optional seal) · Mandatory.
- See Signature Block.

### Footer — Addressee
`To — The Controller of Patents, The Patent Office, at ……`
- **Field F.1 — Patent Office location** — Dropdown (Delhi / Mumbai / Chennai / Kolkata). Mandatory.

## Signature Block

- **Date (Field 4.3)** — Type: Date. Mandatory.
- **Signature (marker ⁴, instruction 4)** — Type: Signature. Mandatory. Signed by the **person(s)
  making the authorisation** (the authorising party, not the agent).
- **Name of the natural person who has signed (marker ⁵, instruction 5)** — Type: Text, **with
  designation and official seal if any**. Mandatory.
- **Place** — not printed; do not invent.

## Special Notes

- **Stamp requirement (footer):** "To be stamped under the Indian Stamp Act, 1899 (2 of 1899)." This is
  a **stamped instrument** — the UI must flag the stamping requirement; export should leave room for
  the stamp. No "See First Schedule" fee note appears on the face (the cost is the stamp duty).
- **Strike-out:** `I/We`, `my/our`.
- **This is the "Power of Authority"** referenced by Form 1 §13(i) — strong linkage: once a Form 26 is
  on file, the agent's details can auto-fill Form 1 §6 and other forms' signatory blocks.
- **Designation & seal:** capture the signatory's designation and official seal where the authoriser is
  an organisation.
