# Form 21

**Official Name:** Request for Termination of Compulsory Licence

**Purpose:** Used to request **termination of a compulsory licence** previously granted by the
Controller, stating the applicant's standing (patentee or a person deriving title/interest), the
grounds, and the documentary evidence.

**Legal Reference:**
- The Patents Act, 1970 (39 of 1970) — **Section 94**
- The Patents Rules, 2003 — **Rule 102(1)**

**Page count:** 1

---

## Overall Layout Notes

Two columns: left = numbered instructions (1–5); right = the form with superscript markers.

---

## Sections

### Section 1 — Applicant Particulars *(instruction 1: name, address and nationality of the applicant(s))*

**Field 1.1 — I/We (name, address, nationality)** *(marker ¹)*
- Field Type: Multi-line Text (composite) · Required: Mandatory · Repeatable: Yes
- Auto-fill: **Yes** — patentee name/address from Certificate (applicant is usually the patentee).
- Validation: Strike-out `I/We`.

### Section 2 — Compulsory Licence to be Terminated

Pre-printed: "hereby apply for the termination of the compulsory licence granted to …… by the order
of the Controller dated …… under Patent No …… dated …… granted to …… for which the patentee ……"

- **Field 2.1 — Compulsory licensee (granted to)** — Text · Mandatory · Auto-fill: **Partial** (from the CL order).
- **Field 2.2 — Controller's order date** — Date · Mandatory · Auto-fill: **Partial**.
- **Field 2.3 — Patent No.** — Text · Mandatory · Auto-fill: **Yes** — from Certificate.
- **Field 2.4 — Dated (of the patent)** — Date · Mandatory · Auto-fill: **Yes**.
- **Field 2.5 — Granted to / patentee** — Text · Mandatory · Auto-fill: **Yes**.

### Section 3 — Standing Declaration (choose applicable)

Two alternative declarations (strike out whichever is not applicable):
- **Field 3.1 — Standing** — Radio/Strike-out:
  - "I/we declare that I am/We are the patentee for the above mentioned patent", **or**
  - "I/we declare that I/we derive title/interest in the patent."
- Required: Mandatory · Auto-fill: No.

### Section 4 — Grounds, Declaration & Evidence

- **Field 4.1 — Grounds for termination** — "on the following grounds, namely:" — Multi-line Text ·
  Mandatory · Repeatable: Yes · Auto-fill: No.
- **Field 4.2 — Truth declaration** — Static/Boilerplate: "I/we declare that the facts and matters
  stated herein are true to the best of my/our knowledge, information and belief." Required: Mandatory.
- **Field 4.3 — Documentary evidence (a)/(b)/(c)** *(marker ²)* — "The details of documentary evidence
  in support of my/our interest and the grounds stated above are given below:" — Text · Mandatory
  (at least one) · Repeatable: Yes · Auto-fill: **Yes** (list uploaded docs).

### Section 5 — Address for Service *(instruction 3; marker ³)*

**Field 5.1 — My/our address for service in India** — Address / Multi-line Text · Mandatory · Auto-fill: from agent.

### Section 6 — Date, Signature & Signatory
`Dated this …… day of …… 20_` — see Signature Block.
- Instruction 4: "To be signed by the applicant(s) or by his authorized registered patent agent."
- Instruction 5 / **Field 6.1 — Name of the natural person who has signed** — Text · Mandatory.

### Footer — Addressee
`To — The Controller of Patents, The Patent Office, At ……`
- **Field F.1 — Patent Office location** — Dropdown (Delhi / Mumbai / Chennai / Kolkata). Mandatory.

## Signature Block

- **Date** — Type: Date. Mandatory.
- **Signature (marker ⁴, instruction 4)** — Type: Signature. Mandatory. Applicant(s) or authorised
  registered patent agent.
- **Name of the natural person who has signed (marker ⁵, instruction 5)** — Type: Text. Mandatory.
- **Place** — not printed; do not invent.

## Special Notes

- **Note (a):** "For fee : See First Schedule." A prescribed fee applies.
- **Note (b):** "Strike out whichever is not applicable." → `I/We`, `I am/We are`, `title/interest`,
  the standing declaration alternatives.
- **Attachment required:** "Certified copies of the documents are to be enclosed **in duplicate**"
  (instruction 2).
