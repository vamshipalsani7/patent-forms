# Form 10

**Official Name:** Application for Amendment of Patent

**Purpose:** Used to request that a **granted patent be amended by substituting the applicant's
name for the name of the grantee** (e.g. to correct/substitute the grantee), supported by
documents. (Section 44 — amendment of patent granted to a deceased applicant / substitution of
grantee.)

**Legal Reference:**
- The Patents Act, 1970 (39 of 1970) — **Section 44**
- The Patents Rules, 2003 — **Rule 75**

**Page count:** 1

---

## Overall Layout Notes

Two columns: left = numbered instructions (1–7); right = the form with superscript markers. The
applicant block (a)–(c) is printed **three times** on the face (space for up to three applicants);
instruction 1 says to repeat columns (a)–(c) for more than one applicant.

---

## Sections

### Section 1 — Applicant(s) Requesting Substitution

Repeatable block (a)–(c); repeat per applicant (instruction 1).

- **Field 1.0 — I/We** *(marker ¹)* — Multi-line Text · Mandatory · Strike-out Choice `I/We`.
- **Field 1.1 — (a) Name** *(marker ²; instruction 2: name in full, family/principal name first)* —
  Text · Mandatory · Repeatable: Yes · Auto-fill: **Yes** — from assignment/probate/records.
- **Field 1.2 — (b) Address** *(marker ³; instruction 3: complete address incl postal index/code,
  state and/or country)* — Address / Multi-line Text · Mandatory · Repeatable: Yes · Auto-fill: **Yes**.
- **Field 1.3 — (c) Nationality** *(marker ⁴; instruction 4)* — Text / Dropdown · Mandatory ·
  Repeatable: Yes · Auto-fill: **Yes**.

### Section 2 — Patent to be Amended

Pre-printed: "hereby request that Patent No.------ dated …… granted to …… may be amended by
substituting my/our name for the name of the grantee and in support to my/our request, I/We furnish
the following documents : ……"

- **Field 2.1 — Patent No.** — Text · Mandatory · Auto-fill: **Yes** — from Certificate.
- **Field 2.2 — Dated (of the patent/grant)** — Date · Mandatory · Auto-fill: **Yes** — grant date.
- **Field 2.3 — Granted to (current grantee)** — Multi-line Text · Mandatory · Auto-fill: **Yes** —
  grantee name from Certificate.
- **Field 2.4 — Documents furnished in support** — Multi-line Text (list) · Mandatory · Repeatable: Yes ·
  Auto-fill: **Yes** — list uploaded supporting documents (assignment, probate, etc.).

### Section 3 — Address for Service *(instruction 5; marker ⁵)*

**Field 3.1 — My/our address for service in India** *(complete address incl postal index number/code
and state along with Telephone and fax number(s))*
- Field Type: Address / Multi-line Text · Required: Mandatory · Auto-fill: from agent/applicant.

### Section 4 — Date, Signature & Signatory
`Dated this …… day of …… 20_` — see Signature Block.
- Instruction 6: "To be signed by the applicant(s) or his authorised registered patent agent."
- Instruction 7 / **Field 4.1 — Name of the natural person who has signed** — Text · Mandatory.

### Footer — Addressee
`To — The Controller of Patents, The Patent Office, At ……`
- **Field F.1 — Patent Office location** — Dropdown (Delhi / Mumbai / Chennai / Kolkata). Mandatory.

## Signature Block

- **Date** — `Dated this … day of … 20_` — Type: Date. Mandatory.
- **Signature (marker ⁶, instruction 6)** — Type: Signature. Mandatory. Signed by applicant(s) or
  authorised registered patent agent.
- **Name of the natural person who has signed (marker ⁷, instruction 7)** — Type: Text. Mandatory.
- **Place** — not printed; do not invent.

---

## Special Notes

- **Fee (footer):** "For fee : See First Schedule." A prescribed fee applies.
- **Strike-out:** `I/We`, `my/our`.
- **Purpose clarity:** this Form 10 amends a **granted patent's grantee name** (substitution). Do not
  confuse with Form 13 (amendment of application/specification/documents) — keep the two editors
  distinct.
- **Attachments required:** documents supporting the substitution (e.g. assignment, legal
  representative/probate documents).
- **Repeatable block:** the (a)–(c) applicant block is pre-printed three times; the editor should
  allow adding/removing applicant rows dynamically rather than hard-capping at three.
