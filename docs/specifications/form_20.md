# Form 20

**Official Name:** Application for Revision of Terms and Conditions of Licence

**Purpose:** Used by a licensee holding a licence granted by the Controller to apply for **revision
of the terms and conditions** of that licence, on the ground that they have proved more onerous than
originally expected and the invention cannot be worked.

**Legal Reference:**
- The Patents Act, 1970 (39 of 1970) — **Section 88(4)**
- The Patents Rules, 2003 — **Rule 100**

**Page count:** 1

---

## Overall Layout Notes

Two columns: left = numbered instructions (1–3); right = the form with a four-part declaration
(i)–(iv). *(OCR note: the source mislabels the third sub-item as "(i)"; the intended sequence is
(i), (ii), (iii), (iv).)*

---

## Sections

### Section 1 — Applicant Particulars *(instruction 1: name, address and nationality of the applicant(s))*

**Field 1.1 — I/We (name, address, nationality)** *(marker ¹)*
- Field Type: Multi-line Text (composite) · Required: Mandatory · Repeatable: Yes
- Auto-fill: **Partial** — applicant is the licensee. Validation: Strike-out `I/We`.

### Section 2 — Declaration (i): The Patent

Pre-printed: "hereby declare: (i) that Patent No. …… dated …… was granted to …… for which the
patentee is ……"
- **Field 2.1 — Patent No.** — Text · Mandatory · Auto-fill: **Yes** — from Certificate.
- **Field 2.2 — Dated (of the patent)** — Date · Mandatory · Auto-fill: **Yes**.
- **Field 2.3 — Granted to** — Text · Mandatory · Auto-fill: **Yes**.
- **Field 2.4 — Patentee** — Text · Mandatory · Auto-fill: **Yes**.

### Section 3 — Declaration (ii): The Licence

Pre-printed: "(ii) that I/We am/are holding licence under the patent, granted by the Controller by
an order dated ……"
- **Field 3.1 — Licence order date** — Date · Mandatory · Auto-fill: **Partial** — from the licence order if uploaded.

### Section 4 — Declaration (iii): Grounds for Revision

Static/Boilerplate: "(iii) that the terms and conditions settled by the Controller have proved to be
more onerous than originally expected and we are unable to work the invention." Required: Mandatory.

### Section 5 — Declaration (iv): Accompanying Statement & Request

- **Field 5.1 — Declaration (iv)** — Static/Boilerplate: "that the circumstances in which this
  application is made are set forth in the accompanying statement in duplicate." Required: Mandatory
  (implies an attachment — see Special Notes).
- **Field 5.2 — Request** — Static/Boilerplate: "I/We request the Controller to revise the terms and
  conditions of the licence." Required: Mandatory.

### Section 6 — Date, Signature & Signatory
`Dated this …… day of …… 20_` — see Signature Block.
- Instruction 2: "To be signed by the applicant(s) or by his authorised registered patent agent."
- Instruction 3 / **Field 6.1 — Name of the natural person who has signed** — Text · Mandatory.

### Footer — Addressee
`To — The Controller of Patents, The Patent Office, At ……`
- **Field F.1 — Patent Office location** — Dropdown (Delhi / Mumbai / Chennai / Kolkata). Mandatory.

## Signature Block

- **Date** — `Dated this … day of … 20_` — Type: Date. Mandatory.
- **Signature (marker ², instruction 2)** — Type: Signature. Mandatory. Applicant(s) or authorised

- **Place** — not printed; do not invent.

## Special Notes

- **Note (a):** "For fee : See First Schedule." A prescribed fee applies.
- **Note (b):** "Strike out whichever is not applicable." → `I/We`, `am/are`.
- **Attachment required:** an accompanying statement (in duplicate) setting out the circumstances
  (declaration (iv)).
- **Precondition:** the applicant must already hold a Controller-granted licence under the patent
  (declaration (ii)); surface this as a check.
