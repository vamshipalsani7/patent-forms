# Form 24

**Official Name:** Application for Review / Setting Aside Controller's Decision / Order

**Purpose:** Used by a party to a proceeding to apply for **review or setting aside** of a decision
or order of the Controller, with the grounds set out in an accompanying statement.

**Legal Reference:**
- The Patents Act, 1970 (39 of 1970) — **Sections 77(1)(f) and 77(1)(g)**
- The Patents Rules, 2003 — **Rules 130(1) and 130(2)**

**Page count:** 1

---

## Overall Layout Notes

Two columns: left = numbered instructions (1–4); right = the form with superscript markers.

---

## Sections

### Section 1 — Matter Reference *(instruction 1: number of patent or patent application and the relevant proceeding)*

**Field 1.1 — In the matter of** *(marker ¹)*
- Field Type: Multi-line Text (patent/application number + relevant proceeding) · Required: Mandatory
- Repeatable: No · Auto-fill: **Partial** — patent/application number from Certificate/Form 1.

### Section 2 — Applicant & Request

**Field 2.1 — I/We (name, address, nationality)** *(instruction... ; marker ²)*
- Field Type: Multi-line Text (composite) · Required: Mandatory · Repeatable: Yes
- Auto-fill: **Partial**. Validation: Strike-out `I/We`.

**Field 2.2 — Capacity in the matter**
- Field Type: Radio/Strike-out (`applicant(s)` | `opponent` | `party`) — "being the
  applicant(s)/opponent/party in the above matter" · Required: Mandatory · Auto-fill: No.

**Field 2.3 — Relief sought**
- Field Type: Radio/Strike-out (`review` | `setting aside`) — "hereby apply for the review/setting
  aside of the Controller's decision/order dated the ……" · Required: Mandatory · Auto-fill: No.

**Field 2.4 — Controller's decision/order date**
- Field Type: Date · Required: Mandatory · Auto-fill: **Partial** — from the order if uploaded.

**Field 2.5 — Grounds statement reference**
- Field Type: Static/Boilerplate: "The grounds for making the application are set forth in the
  accompanying statement submitted in duplicate." Required: Mandatory (implies an attachment).

### Section 3 — Date, Signature & Signatory
`Dated this …… day of …… 20_` — see Signature Block.
- Instruction 3: "To be signed by the applicant(s) or his authorized registered patent agent."
- Instruction 4 / **Field 3.1 — Name of the natural person who has signed** — Text · Mandatory.

### Footer — Addressee
`To — The Controller of Patents, The Patent Office, at ……`
- **Field F.1 — Patent Office location** — Dropdown (Delhi / Mumbai / Chennai / Kolkata). Mandatory.

## Signature Block

- **Date** — `Dated this … day of … 20_` — Type: Date. Mandatory.
- **Signature (marker ³, instruction 3)** — Type: Signature. Mandatory. Applicant(s) or authorised
  registered patent agent.
- **Name of the natural person who has signed (marker ⁴, instruction 4)** — Type: Text. Mandatory.
- **Place** — not printed; do not invent.

---

## Special Notes

- **Fee (footer):** "For fee : See First Schedule." A prescribed fee applies.
- **Strike-out:** `I/We`, `applicant(s)/opponent/party`, `review/setting aside`, `decision/order`.
- **Attachment required:** a statement of grounds, submitted **in duplicate**.
- **Time-bar warning (UI):** review petitions under Rule 130 are time-bound; surface the deadline.
