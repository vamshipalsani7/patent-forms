# Form 9

**Official Name:** Request for Publication

**Purpose:** Used by an applicant to request **early publication** of a patent application (ahead
of the ordinary 18-month publication), under Section 11A(2).

**Legal Reference:**
- The Patents Act, 1970 (39 of 1970) — **Section 11A(2)**
- The Patents Rules, 2003 — **Rule 24A**

**Page count:** 1

---

## Overall Layout Notes

Two columns: left = numbered instructions (1–3); right = the form with superscript markers. Short,
single-purpose form.

---

## Sections

### Section 1 — Applicant Particulars *(instruction 1: name, address and nationality of the applicant(s))*

**Field 1.1 — I/We (name, address, nationality)** *(marker ¹)*
- Field Type: Multi-line Text (composite) · Required: Mandatory · Repeatable: Yes
- Auto-fill: **Yes** — applicant name/address/nationality from Form 1 / Specification.
- Validation: Strike-out Choice `I/We`.

### Section 2 — Application to be Published

Pre-printed: "hereby request for early publication of my/our Patent application No …… dated ……
under section 11A(2) of the Act."

- **Field 2.1 — Patent application No.** — Text · Mandatory · Auto-fill: **Yes** — from Form 1 / ack.
- **Field 2.2 — Dated (of the application)** — Date · Mandatory · Auto-fill: **Yes** — filing date.

### Section 3 — Date, Signature & Signatory

- **Field 3.0 — Dated this __ day of __ 20__** — Date · Mandatory · Auto-fill: derived (signing date).
- Instruction 2: "To be signed by the applicant or his authorized registered patent agent."
- Instruction 3 / **Field 3.1 — Name of the natural person who has signed** — Text · Mandatory.
- See Signature Block.

### Footer — Addressee
`To — The Controller of Patents, The Patent Office, At ……`
- **Field F.1 — Patent Office location** — Dropdown (Delhi / Mumbai / Chennai / Kolkata). Mandatory.

## Signature Block

- **Date (Field 3.0)** — Type: Date. Mandatory.
- **Signature (marker ²)** — Type: Signature. Mandatory. Signed by applicant or authorised
  registered patent agent.
- **Name of the natural person who has signed (marker ³)** — Type: Text. Mandatory.
- **Place** — not printed; do not invent.

---

## Special Notes

- **Fee (footer):** "For fee : See First Schedule." A prescribed fee applies for early publication.
- **Strike-out:** `I/We`, `my/our`.
- **Strong auto-fill:** almost every field derives from Form 1 — this is a good candidate for
  near-complete pre-fill, leaving only confirmation and signature.
- **Attachments:** none prescribed on the face.
