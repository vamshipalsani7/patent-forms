# Form 19

**Official Name:** Application for Revocation of a Patent for Non Working

**Purpose:** Used to apply for **revocation of a patent on the ground of non-working** (failure to
work the patented invention in India), stating the applicant's interest, the reasons/grounds, and
the documentary evidence relied upon.

**Legal Reference:**
- The Patents Act, 1970 (39 of 1970) — **Section 85(1)**
- The Patents Rules, 2003 — **Rule 96**

**Page count:** 1

---

## Overall Layout Notes

Two columns: left = numbered instructions (1–6); right = the form with superscript markers.

---

## Sections

### Section 1 — Applicant Particulars *(instruction 1: name, address and nationality of the applicant(s))*

**Field 1.1 — I/We (name, address, nationality)** *(marker ¹)*
- Field Type: Multi-line Text (composite) · Required: Mandatory · Repeatable: Yes
- Auto-fill: **Partial** — applicant is typically an interested third party. Validation: Strike-out `I/We`.

### Section 2 — Patent to be Revoked & Reason

Pre-printed: "hereby apply for revocation of Patent No. …… dated …… granted to …… for which the
patentee/applicant for patent is …… for the following reason, namely:² ……"

- **Field 2.1 — Patent No.** — Text · Mandatory · Auto-fill: **Yes** — from Certificate.
- **Field 2.2 — Dated (of the patent)** — Date · Mandatory · Auto-fill: **Yes**.
- **Field 2.3 — Granted to** — Text · Mandatory · Auto-fill: **Yes**.
- **Field 2.4 — Patentee / applicant for patent** — Text · Mandatory · Auto-fill: **Yes** (Strike-out `patentee/applicant`).
- **Field 2.5 — Reason(s) / grounds** *(instruction 2: nature of interest, facts relied on, grounds; marker ²)* —
  Multi-line Text · Mandatory · Repeatable: Yes · Auto-fill: No.

### Section 3 — Documentary Evidence *(instruction 3; marker ³)*

Pre-printed: "The details of documentary evidence in support of my/our interest and the reasons
stated above are given below:³ (a) …… (b) …… (c) ……"
- **Field 3.1 — Evidence item (a)/(b)/(c)** — Text · Mandatory (at least one) · Repeatable: Yes ·
  Auto-fill: **Yes** — list uploaded supporting documents.

### Section 4 — Declaration & Address for Service *(instruction 4; marker ⁴)*

- **Field 4.1 — Declaration** — Static/Boilerplate: "I/We declare that the facts and matters stated
  herein are true to the best of my/our knowledge, information and belief." Required: Mandatory.
- **Field 4.2 — My/Our address for service in India** *(complete address incl postal index number/code
  and state along with telephone and fax number(s))* — Address / Multi-line Text · Mandatory · Auto-fill: from agent.

### Section 5 — Date, Signature & Signatory
`Dated this …… day of …… 20_` — see Signature Block.
- Instruction 5: "To be signed by the applicant(s) or his authorized registered patent agent."
- Instruction 6 / **Field 5.1 — Name of the natural person who has signed** — Text · Mandatory.

### Footer — Addressee
`To — The Controller of Patents, The Patent Office, At ……`
- **Field F.1 — Patent Office location** — Dropdown (Delhi / Mumbai / Chennai / Kolkata). Mandatory.

## Signature Block

- **Date** — `Dated this … day of … 20_` — Type: Date. Mandatory.
- **Signature (marker ⁵, instruction 5)** — Type: Signature. Mandatory. Applicant(s) or authorised
  registered patent agent.
- **Name of the natural person who has signed (marker ⁶, instruction 6)** — Type: Text. Mandatory.
- **Place** — not printed; do not invent.

---

## Special Notes

- **Note (a):** "For fee : See First Schedule." A prescribed fee applies.
- **Note (b):** "Strike out whichever is not applicable." → `I/We`, `my/our`, `patentee/applicant`.
- **Attachments required:** "Certified copies of all the documents are to be enclosed **in duplicate**"
  (instruction 3).
- **Nature of interest required:** instruction 2 requires the applicant to state the nature of their
  interest, the facts relied on, and the grounds — captured in Field 2.5.
