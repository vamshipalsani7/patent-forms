# Form 14

**Official Name:** Notice of Opposition to Amendment / Restoration / Surrender of Patent / Grant of
Compulsory Licence or Revision of Terms Thereof or to Correction of Clerical Errors

**Purpose:** A single notice-of-opposition form covering **six** distinct opposition scenarios,
selected by the opponent: opposition to an amendment, to restoration, to surrender, to grant of a
compulsory licence or revocation, to revision of licence terms, or to correction of a clerical error.

**Legal Reference:**
- The Patents Act, 1970 (39 of 1970) — **Sections 57(4), 61(1), 63(3), 78(5) and 87(2)**
- The Patents Rules, 2003 — **Rules 81(3)(b), 85(1), 87(2), 98(1), 101(3) and 124**

**Page count:** 1

---

## Overall Layout Notes

Two columns: left = numbered instructions (1–4); right = the form with superscript markers. The
right column presents **six mutually exclusive opposition types joined by "OR"** — the opponent
selects one and completes its patent reference.

---

## Sections

### Section 1 — Opponent Particulars *(instruction 1: name, address and nationality)*

**Field 1.1 — I/We (name, address, nationality)** *(marker ¹)*
- Field Type: Multi-line Text (composite) · Required: Mandatory · Repeatable: Yes
- Auto-fill: **Partial** — opponent may be a third party. Validation: Strike-out Choice `I/We`.

### Section 2 — Type of Opposition (choose one of six)

**Field 2.1 — Opposition type**
- Field Type: Radio Button (single select), one of:
  1. to the **amendment** of the application/specification with respect to application for Patent No. …… dated ……
  2. to the application for **restoration** of Patent No. …… dated ……
  3. to the offer to **surrender** the Patent No. …… dated ……
  4. for the grant of **compulsory licence, or revocation** of Patent No. …… dated ……
  5. for the **revision of the terms and conditions of licence** in respect of Patent No. …… dated ……
  6. for **correction of a clerical error** in Patent No. …… dated …… / Specification No. …… dated …… in
     respect of Patent No. …… dated …… or Patent application No. …… dated ……
- Required?: Mandatory · Repeatable?: No · Auto-fill: **Partial** — infer from the underlying
  proceeding if that document was uploaded; user confirms.

**Field 2.2 — Patent / application / specification No.(s)** (conditional on the chosen type)
- Field Type: Text (one or more numbers depending on the selected type — option 6 has multiple)
- Required?: Conditional (Mandatory for the selected type) · Auto-fill: **Yes** — from Certificate / Form 1.

**Field 2.3 — Dated (of each referenced patent/application/specification)**
- Field Type: Date (one per referenced number) · Required?: Conditional · Auto-fill: **Yes**.

### Section 3 — Grounds

**Field 3.1 — Grounds of opposition** — "The grounds in which the said opposition is made are as follows:"
- Field Type: Multi-line Text · Required?: Mandatory · Repeatable?: Yes (one per ground) · Auto-fill: No.

### Section 4 — Address for Service *(instruction 2; marker ²)*

**Field 4.1 — My / Our address for service in India** *(complete address incl postal index number/
code and state along with Telephone and fax number(s))*
- Field Type: Address / Multi-line Text · Required: Mandatory · Auto-fill: from opponent's agent.

### Section 5 — Date, Signature & Signatory
`Dated this …… day of …… 20_` — see Signature Block.
- Instruction 3: "To be signed by the opponent or his authorised registered patent agent."
- Instruction 4 / **Field 5.1 — Name of the natural person who has signed** — Text · Mandatory.

### Footer — Addressee
`To — The Controller of Patents, The Patent Office, At ……`
- **Field F.1 — Patent Office location** — Dropdown (Delhi / Mumbai / Chennai / Kolkata). Mandatory.

## Signature Block

- **Date** — `Dated this … day of … 20_` — Type: Date. Mandatory.
- **Signature (marker ³, instruction 3)** — Type: Signature. Mandatory. Opponent or authorised
  registered patent agent.
- **Name of the natural person who has signed (marker ⁴, instruction 4)** — Type: Text. Mandatory.
- **Place** — not printed; do not invent.

---

## Special Notes

- **Note (a):** "Strike out whichever is not applicable." → the six OR-branches; only the selected
  branch is retained, the rest struck out on export.
- **Note (b):** "For fee : See First Schedule." A prescribed fee applies.
- **Six-in-one form:** the editor must render only the fields for the chosen opposition type and adjust
  the patent-reference inputs accordingly (option 6 needs several numbers/dates).
- **Attachments:** written statement and evidence typically accompany, per the relevant opposition rule.
