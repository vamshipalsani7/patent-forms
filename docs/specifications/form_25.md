# Form 25

**Official Name:** Request for Permission for Making Patent Application Outside India

**Purpose:** Used by a person resident in India to request the Controller's **permission to file a
patent application outside India** for the same or substantially the same invention, under Section 39.

**Legal Reference:**
- The Patents Act, 1970 (39 of 1970) — **Section 39**
- The Patents Rules, 2003 — **Rule 71(1)**

**Page count:** 1

---

## Overall Layout Notes

Two columns: left = numbered instructions (1–4); right = the form with superscript markers. The
right column has an **"OR"** branch on whether an India application already exists or a brief
description is attached instead.

---

## Sections

### Section 1 — Title & Existing India Application *(instruction 1: state the title of the invention)*

Pre-printed: "I am/We are in possession of an invention for¹ …… I/We have made an application for the
grant of a patent for the said invention, its number being No …… of …… Dated …… **OR** I/we hereby
attach the brief description of the invention."

- **Field 1.1 — Title of the invention** *(marker ¹)* — Multi-line Text · Mandatory · Auto-fill: **Yes** (Form 2 / Form 1 §5).
- **Field 1.2 — Existing-application branch** — Radio Button (`India application already made` |
  `brief description attached`) · Mandatory · Auto-fill: **Partial**.
- **Field 1.3 — India application number** — Text · Conditional (branch A) · Auto-fill: **Yes** (Form 1).
- **Field 1.4 — Of (year) / Dated** — Date · Conditional (branch A) · Auto-fill: **Yes** (filing date).
- **Field 1.5 — Brief description of the invention** — Multi-line Text / attachment · Conditional (branch B) · Auto-fill: **Yes** (from specification).

### Section 2 — Intended Foreign Filing *(instruction 2: name and address of the person(s); marker ²)*

Pre-printed: "I/We intend to make application(s) **alone/jointly with²** …… for the **same/substantially
same** invention for patent in the following **country/countries/convention countries**, namely:- ……"

- **Field 2.1 — Alone / jointly with** — Strike-out Choice (`alone` | `jointly with`) · Mandatory.
- **Field 2.2 — Joint person(s) name & address** — Multi-line Text · Conditional (if jointly) · Auto-fill: **Partial**.
- **Field 2.3 — Country/countries for intended filing** — Text / Multi-select (countries) · Mandatory ·
  Repeatable: Yes · Auto-fill: No.

### Section 3 — Assignment Declaration *(instruction 3: name and address of the assignee; marker ³)*

Pre-printed: "I/We declare that the rights in the application(s) **has/have** been assigned to³ ……"
- **Field 3.1 — Assignee name & address** — Address / Multi-line Text · Conditional (if assigned) · Auto-fill: **Partial**.

### Section 4 — Request, Reasons & Declaration

- **Field 4.1 — Permission request** — Static/Boilerplate: "I/We request that I/We may be granted
  permission to make application(s) for the said invention in the said country/countries." Required: Mandatory.
- **Field 4.2 — Reasons for making this application** — Multi-line Text · Mandatory · Auto-fill: No.
- **Field 4.3 — Truth declaration** — Static/Boilerplate: "The facts and matters stated above are true
  to the best of my/our knowledge, information and belief." Required: Mandatory.
- **Field 4.4 — Dated this __ day of __ 20__** — Date · Mandatory · Auto-fill: derived.

### Section 5 — Signature
- Instruction 4: "To be signed by the applicant(s) or authorised patent agent." See Signature Block.

### Footer — Addressee
`To — The Controller of Patents, The Patent Office, at ……`
- **Field F.1 — Patent Office location** — Dropdown (Delhi / Mumbai / Chennai / Kolkata). Mandatory.

## Special Notes

- **Note (a):** "Strike out whichever is not applicable." → `I am/We are`, `alone/jointly`,
  `same/substantially same`, `country/countries/convention countries`, `has/have`.
- **Note (b):** "For fee : See First Schedule." A prescribed fee applies.
- **Purpose/warning (UI):** Section 39 requires prior permission (or a 6-week wait after an India
  filing) before filing abroad; foreign filing without it can invalidate the Indian patent. Surface
  this prominently — it is a high-stakes compliance step.
- **Attachment (branch B):** a brief description of the invention where no India application exists yet.
