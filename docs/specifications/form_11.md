# Form 11

**Official Name:** Application for Direction of the Controller

**Purpose:** Used to apply for a direction of the Controller in respect of a patent (e.g.
directions concerning co-ownership / rights of joint patentees), stating the direction sought
and the reasons.

**Legal Reference:**
- The Patents Act, 1970 (39 of 1970) — **Sections 51(1) and 51(2)**
- The Patents Rules, 2003 — **Rules 76 and 77**

**Page count:** 1

---

## Overall Layout Notes

Two columns: left = numbered instructions (1–4); right = the form with superscript markers.

---

## Sections

### Section 1 — Applicant Particulars *(instruction 1: name in full, address and nationality)*

**Field 1.1 — I/We (name, address, nationality)** *(marker ¹)*
- Field Type: Multi-line Text (composite) · Required: Mandatory · Repeatable: Yes
- Auto-fill: **Yes** — applicant name/address/nationality from Form 1 / Certificate.
- Validation: Strike-out Choice `I/We`.

### Section 2 — Direction Sought

Pre-printed: "hereby apply for the following direction in respect of patent No. ------ dated ……
grant to ------ ……"

- **Field 2.1 — Patent No.** — Text · Mandatory · Auto-fill: **Yes** — from Certificate.
- **Field 2.2 — Dated (of the patent)** — Date · Mandatory · Auto-fill: **Yes** — grant date.
- **Field 2.3 — Granted to** — Multi-line Text · Mandatory · Auto-fill: **Yes** — patentee name(s).
- **Field 2.4 — Direction applied for** — Multi-line Text · Mandatory · Repeatable: No · Auto-fill: No.

### Section 3 — Reasons

**Field 3.1 — Reasons for making this application** — Multi-line Text · Mandatory · Auto-fill: No.

### Section 4 — Address for Service *(instruction 2; marker ²)*

**Field 4.1 — My/Our address for service in India** *(complete address incl postal index number/code
and state along with Telephone and fax number(s))*
- Field Type: Address / Multi-line Text · Required: Mandatory · Auto-fill: from agent/applicant.

### Section 5 — Date, Signature & Signatory
`Dated this …… day of …… 20_` — see Signature Block.
- Instruction 3: "To be signed by the applicant(s) or his authorised registered patent agent."
- Instruction 4 / **Field 5.1 — Name of the natural person who has signed** — Text · Mandatory.

### Footer — Addressee
`To — The Controller of Patents, The Patent Office, At ……`
- **Field F.1 — Patent Office location** — Dropdown (Delhi / Mumbai / Chennai / Kolkata). Mandatory.

## Signature Block

- **Date** — `Dated this … day of … 20_` — Type: Date. Mandatory.
- **Signature (marker ³, instruction 3)** — Type: Signature. Mandatory. Applicant(s) or authorised
  registered patent agent.
- **Name of the natural person who has signed (marker ⁴, instruction 4)** — Type: Text. Mandatory.
- **Place** — not printed; do not invent.

---

## Special Notes

- **Fee (footer):** "For fee : See First Schedule." A prescribed fee applies.
- **Strike-out:** `I/We`, `My/Our`.
- **Context:** Section 51 concerns Controller's directions to co-owners of a patent; the "direction
  applied for" free-text captures the specific relief sought.
