# Form 13

**Official Name:** Application for Amendment of the Application for Patent / Complete Specification /
Any Document Related Thereto

**Purpose:** Used to request leave to **amend** the patent application, the complete specification,
or any document related thereto, with the proposed amendments highlighted in an annexed copy, and
including declarations that no infringement/revocation action is pending and that the stated facts
are true.

**Legal Reference:**
- The Patents Act, 1970 (39 of 1970) — **Section 57**
- The Patents Rules, 2003 — **Rule 81, sub-rule (1)**

**Page count:** 1

---

## Overall Layout Notes

Two-column table with three numbered rows plus footer. Row 1's right column is a single pre-printed
paragraph containing the amendment request, the reasons, and two mandatory declarations.

## Special Notes

- **Fee (footer):** `Note.- For fee: See First Schedule.` A prescribed fee applies.
- **Strike-out:** `I/We`, `my/our`, and the amendment-subject choice (`application / any document /
  complete specification`).
- **Required attachment — highlighted copy:** the proposed amendments must be shown "as highlighted
  in the copy hereto annexed"; the app should require/attach a marked-up copy of the amended document.
- **Two mandatory declarations:** (a) no pending infringement/revocation action; (b) truth of facts.
- **Distinguish from Form 10:** Form 13 amends the **application/specification/documents** (s.57);
  Form 10 amends a **granted patent's grantee name** (s.44). Keep the editors separate.
- **Signatory scope:** may be signed by applicant(s) **or patentee(s)** (pre- or post-grant amendment).
